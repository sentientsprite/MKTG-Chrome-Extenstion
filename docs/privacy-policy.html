Privacy Policy

AI Growth Coach · Last updated June 30, 2026

Summary: AI Growth Coach runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate backend servers. Your marketing data is fetched directly from Google APIs. AI features use your own OpenAI API key — requests go directly from your device to OpenAI, not through us.

What data we access

When you sign in with Google and configure data sources, the extension may read:

  • Google Analytics 4 — traffic, engagement, and conversion metrics
  • Google Search Console — organic search clicks, impressions, rankings
  • Google Ads (optional) — ad spend, clicks, conversions
  • Google Business Profile (optional) — reviews, ratings, listing performance
  • Google account — email and name for display in the UI

OpenAI — bring your own key

AI Coach features require an OpenAI API key that you provide in Settings. Marketing metric summaries are sent directly from your browser to OpenAI. We do not proxy, log, or store these requests. You are billed by OpenAI per your account terms. OpenAI Privacy Policy

What we do not do

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Run tracking or analytics on users
  • Modify your Google marketing accounts (read-only access)
  • Store your data on servers we control

Your choices

  • Sign out in Settings to revoke OAuth tokens
  • Remove your OpenAI API key to disable AI features
  • Revoke access at Google Account permissions
  • Uninstall the extension to delete all local data
